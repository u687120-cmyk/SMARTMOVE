How data comes in

✋ ManualNo hardware. Tap Report → type → submit. Works on any phone or laptop.
📱 Phone sensorStart trip. Accelerometer + GPS detect jolts above 15 km/h and report automatically. Needs HTTPS or localhost.
📷 Phone cameraAdd a photo to any report. During a trip the camera can auto-snap a frame when the sensor fires.
🔌 External hardwareDashcam, OBD or IMU units send data to your backend or call SmartMove.report({type,lat,lng}). Same pipeline, no UI change.

Verification

A hazard becomes Verified when 3 different devices confirm it within 20 m. One phone can never verify alone. Verified hazards raise route risk more than single reports.
